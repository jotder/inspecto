# Telecom industry pack — features

**Product Map Item:** [`solution-pack.industry-telecom`](../../../../../product-map/solution-pack/industry-telecom.toon) · **Status:** ga · **Availability:** Billable Industry pack `INS-IP-TELECOM`, licensed by contract; bundled in every Edition from Basic as part of the Link Analysis base.

## What it is

The `telecom` Industry pack: lines, identities and handsets linked by calls, messages, forwarding and shared devices, as Link Analysis vocabulary.

## Who benefits

Telecom fraud and security analysts using Link Analysis.

## Key capabilities

- 6 telecom Entity Types (subscriber, MSISDN, IMSI, IMEI, handset, cell) with normalisers and masking — [entity-store](../../module/entity-store/index.md)
- 6 link kinds and 20 relabelled terms
- 4 pattern packs: forwarding relay, forwarding chain, payment ring, subscription ring — [la-core](../../module/la-core/index.md)
- "Investigate a number" defaults keyed on MSISDN
- Paired Space Templates: [Telecom fraud](../telco-fraud/index.md) and [Link Analysis showcase](../la-showcase/index.md)
