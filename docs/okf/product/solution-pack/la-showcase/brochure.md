# Link Analysis showcase — brochure

> **Watch a mule ring, a shell-company loop and five telecom fraud rings surface from plain data in one guided walk-through.**

*Audience: customers, presales and sales. Product Map Item `solution-pack.la-showcase` · status **preview**. This is a seeded demonstration Space on synthetic data, not a sold product. Prices are not published here.*

## Who it is for

Presales teams and prospects evaluating Link Analysis for financial-crime or telecom fraud.

## The problems it solves

- Showing, not telling, how Link Analysis finds a ring hidden among ordinary activity — with governance (masking, four-eyes, Drafts) visible.

## What is included

- A daily transfer feed with three landed days plus staged next-day and gap-day files; a planted mule ring that the Suspicion score ranks in the top three.
- A telecom corpus of eight 60-day feeds with one planted ring per typology (Wangiri, IRSF, SIM box, subscription fraud, mules).
- A geo scene with three saved Geo Views.
- Link Analysis settings with masking, four-eyes, the link index and Drafts; index-build and standing-detection Jobs; saved views.
- Five demo users and a role table; a seeding script for Investigations and a Case.

## How it fits with the Editions

Needs an [Advanced](../../edition/advanced/brochure.md) or [Preview](../../edition/preview/brochure.md) build with demo sign-in. Companion: [Link Analysis showcase (feature off)](../../solution-pack/la-showcase-off/brochure.md).

## How it works

Create the Space from the template, run the seeding script against the running server, and sign in as the analyst, the manager or the reviewer to walk the story from Alert to Investigation to Dossier.
