# Orders starter — brochure

> **Your first live Space in minutes — a feed, a quality check, a Dataset and a dashboard, already wired together.**

*Audience: customers, presales and sales. Product Map Item `solution-pack.orders-starter` · status **ga**. Prices are not published here.*

## Who it is for

New customers and evaluators learning how Inspecto works, and teams who want a clean pattern to copy for their own first feed.

## The problems it solves

- Getting from "we installed it" to "we see our data on a dashboard" without a services engagement.

## What is included

- A retail-orders Pipeline with schema and a derived gross column (three days of sample files).
- A non-null quality Expectation.
- A queryable Dataset.
- A dashboard with a KPI and a revenue-by-region chart.

## How it fits with the Editions

Ships with every Edition from [Basic](../../edition/basic/brochure.md) up.

## How it works

Create a Space from the template, copy the sample files into the inbox, and watch the Collector land them, the Expectation check them and the dashboard fill. Then replace the sample feed with your own.

## Next step

Use it as step one of any trial, then move to the solution pack for your domain.
