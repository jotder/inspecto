# Orders starter — features

**Product Map Item:** [`solution-pack.orders-starter`](../../../../../product-map/solution-pack/orders-starter.toon) · **Status:** ga · **Availability:** Billable content pack `INS-SP-ORDERS-STARTER`, every Edition from Basic (`offerings/basic.toon` `contentPacks`).

## What it is

A Space Template (`orders-starter`) with everything a first Space needs over a 3-day retail-orders CSV feed.

## Who benefits

New customers and evaluators, and teams that want a pattern to copy for their own first feed.

## Key capabilities

- Stage-1 Pipeline with Schema and a derived `GROSS` column — [etl](../../module/etl/index.md)
- Non-null quality Expectation on `ORDER_ID`
- Queryable Dataset `orders_dataset`
- Dashboard `orders_overview`: total-orders KPI and a revenue-by-region bar chart
- Three days of sample files
