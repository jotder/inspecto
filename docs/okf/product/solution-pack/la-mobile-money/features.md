# Link Analysis mobile money — features

**Product Map Item:** [`solution-pack.la-mobile-money`](../../../../../product-map/solution-pack/la-mobile-money.toon) · **Status:** preview · **Availability:** not billable; Space Template `la-mobile-money`, needs an Advanced or Preview build with Demo Auth.

## What it is

A demonstration Space on synthetic wallet data: 420 wallets, 40 agents, 40 merchants and about 5 700 transactions over 14 days. Ground truths are pinned by `LaMobileMoneyGoldenTest` and `MmLinksJobGoldenTest`.

## Who benefits

Presales teams and mobile-money operators.

## Key capabilities

- Agent-assisted structuring: 25 wallets just under the Tier-1 limit through one agent (the `structuring` Value Measure).
- Mule chain: 98 % pass-through to a cash-out agent (shortest path, maximum flow, `passThrough` Measure).
- Device farm: nine wallets on one handset with nine different ids.
- The only directed cycle: a four-wallet round trip.
- Dormant wallet awakening against the masked KYC reference.
- Benign biller hub that tops degree and Suspicion score anyway.
- The `mm-structuring` Alert (aggregate only) to Incident and Case.
- Typed masking, four-eyes above 100 neighbours, Drafts, Dossier, and a regulator embed portal.
