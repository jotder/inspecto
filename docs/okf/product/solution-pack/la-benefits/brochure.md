# Link Analysis benefits and tax fraud showcase — brochure

> **An address farm, pensions still paid to the dead, jobseekers on a payroll, and a tax preparer steering 80 inflated refunds into six accounts, hidden among 12 000 ordinary people. Find each one from a saved view in minutes, and see why the innocent look-alikes stay apart.**

*Audience: customers, presales and sales. Product Map Item `solution-pack.la-benefits` · status **preview**. This is a seeded demonstration Space on synthetic data, not a sold product. Prices are not published here.*

## Who it is for

Government benefits-integrity and tax-compliance teams, and presales teams who want to show Link Analysis on claim, death-register, payroll and tax-return data.

## The problems it solves

- **Address farm:** many claimants give one address, each declaring a household of one, and their benefits land in a handful of accounts.
- **Payments to the deceased:** pensions keep being paid after the death, into accounts that someone alive controls.
- **Undeclared work:** people on Jobseeker benefit appear on employers' payroll at full wages, month after month.
- **Tax-preparer ring:** one preparer files dozens of returns with inflated refunds, all paid into accounts the preparer controls.

Each story has an innocent look-alike that a simple threshold check alone would flag: care homes with dozens of genuine residents, a death notified weeks late whose overpayment was recovered, a jobseeker with permitted part-time earnings, and a large preparer whose clients are refunded into their own accounts. The graph shows the difference.

## What is included

- Seven synthetic feeds (addresses, people, household declarations, claims, the death register, payroll, tax returns): twelve months landed and one more month to drop in during the demo.
- Seven Collectors and Pipelines, a Dataset per feed, one link Dataset of six link kinds built by a Job after every load, and one derived Dataset of four integrity screens.
- Four saved views, one per story, each small enough to draw whole.
- A user guide with a five-minute tour and one exercise per story, with answers.

## How it fits with the Editions

Runs on every edition, [Basic](../../edition/basic/brochure.md) included: no sign-in, Alert Rules or Investigations are needed. An Advanced tier (personas, an Alert Rule for a payment into an account linked to a death-register entry, a seeding script) is planned.

## How it works

Download the product, the Space and the data, unzip them side by side, and start the product. The Pipelines load the data on start-up; open a saved view and expand from its busiest node.
