# Mobile money industry pack — features

**Product Map Item:** [`solution-pack.industry-mobile-money`](../../../../../product-map/solution-pack/industry-mobile-money.toon) · **Status:** ga · **Availability:** Billable Industry pack `INS-IP-MOBILE-MONEY`, licensed by contract; bundled in every Edition from Basic.

## What it is

The `mobile-money` Industry pack: wallets and agents linked by transfers, cash-in, cash-out and merchant payments, as Link Analysis vocabulary.

## Who benefits

Mobile-money fraud, AML and assurance analysts using Link Analysis.

## Key capabilities

- 3 Entity Types (wallet, agent, MSISDN) with normalisers and masking — [entity-store](../../module/entity-store/index.md)
- 6 link kinds and 16 relabelled terms
- 5 pattern packs: pass-through, layering chain, inbound collector, circular flow, structuring — [la-core](../../module/la-core/index.md)
- Amount and time hints and suggested tools (scoring, pattern, flow)
- Paired Space Templates: [Mobile money](../mobile-money/index.md) and [Link Analysis mobile money](../la-mobile-money/index.md)
