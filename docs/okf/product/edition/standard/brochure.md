# Inspecto Standard — brochure

> **A governed assurance and investigation platform for a team — sign-in, Cases, Reconciliations, scoring and Investigations with evidence you can defend.**

*Audience: customers, presales and sales. Product Map Item `edition.standard` · status **ga**. Prices are not published here.*

## Who it is for

Revenue-assurance, fraud, AML and data-quality teams that need several analysts working on the same data with roles, an audit trail and controls a regulator or auditor will accept.

## The problems it solves

- Leakage and fraud are found but not followed up — there is no Incident, Case or SLA behind an Alert.
- Two systems should agree (usage vs billing, wallet vs bank) and nobody can prove they do.
- Investigations end in screenshots that cannot be verified later.
- Sensitive identifiers are visible to everyone who opens a screen.

## How it fits with the Editions

Standard includes all of [Basic](../../edition/basic/brochure.md). [Advanced](../../edition/advanced/brochure.md) adds organisation-scale governance on top of Standard. Standard is where every solution pack except the Link Analysis showcases runs.

## How it works

Data flows in as in Basic, and every finding now has somewhere to go: an Alert Rule raises Incidents, Incidents group into Cases with owners and SLAs, Reconciliations prove two feeds agree, Risk Scores and Anomaly Models rank what to look at first, and Investigations record each step so a Dossier can be verified byte for byte later. Users sign in through your existing identity provider; Inspecto never stores passwords.
