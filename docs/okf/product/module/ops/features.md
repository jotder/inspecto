# Case management and objects — features

**Product Map Item:** [`module.ops`](../../../../../product-map/module/ops.toon) · **Status:** ga · **Availability:** Optional module, staged from Standard and every higher Edition (`offerings/standard.toon`). Add-on: [incidents](../../addon/incidents/index.md), [workflowSla](../../addon/workflowSla/index.md).

## What it is

Operational objects are the Alerts, Incidents, Cases and Tasks the platform raises, with notes, links and tags. They share one mail-like three-pane screen and a workflow-driven lifecycle.

## Who benefits

Operations and assurance teams triaging what the platform raises.

## Key capabilities

- Mail-like three-pane screen for Incidents and Cases — [user-guide-objects](user-guide-objects.md)
- Workflow, SLA policy and escalation config kinds with a background SLA sweep — [addon workflowSla feature-db](../../addon/workflowSla/feature-db.md)
- `create-incident` consequence and Incident purge maintenance — [addon incidents feature-db](../../addon/incidents/feature-db.md)
