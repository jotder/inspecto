# Attribute-based access policy — features

**Product Map Item:** [`module.policy`](../../../../../product-map/module/policy.toon) · **Status:** ga · **Availability:** Optional module, staged from Advanced and every higher Edition (`offerings/advanced.toon`).

## What it is

Attribute-based access policy supplies the `PolicyEngine` that decides access from attributes, not just roles. It also carries the `PII_MASK` edition feature.

## Who benefits

Regulated customers who need row-level and attribute-level access control and masking of personal data.

## Key capabilities

- ABAC decisions behind the `AccessDecider` contract, plus PII masking — [Identity and Access](../access/features-access.md)
