# Case Management — features

**Product Map Item:** [`addon.caseManagement`](../../../../../product-map/addon/caseManagement.toon) · **Status:** ga · **Availability:** Billable add-on `INS-AO-CASE-MANAGEMENT`, offered from Standard and every higher Edition (`offerings/standard.toon`); it stages [case-management](../../module/case-management/index.md).

## What it is

Case Management groups related Incidents into Cases for investigation. Case grouping is evaluated by the `caserule.evaluate` job type.

## Who benefits

Investigation teams who work a problem as one Case rather than many alerts.
