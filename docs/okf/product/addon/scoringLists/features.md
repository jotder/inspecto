# Scoring & Lists — features

**Product Map Item:** [`addon.scoringLists`](../../../../../product-map/addon/scoringLists.toon) · **Status:** ga · **Availability:** Billable add-on `INS-AO-SCORING-LISTS`, offered from Standard and every higher Edition (`offerings/standard.toon`); it stages [entity-list](../../module/entity-list/index.md), [scoring](../../module/scoring/index.md).

## What it is

Scoring & Lists combines Entity Lists with Risk Scores. Lists say who to watch; scores say how risky an entity looks.

## Who benefits

Fraud and AML analysts prioritising work.

## Key capabilities

- `risk-score` config kind and `risk.score` job type — [feature-db](feature-db.md)
- Entity Lists with ranges, expiry and maker-checker — [entity-list](../../module/entity-list/index.md)
- Risk Scores — [scoring spec](../../module/scoring/spec-risk-scores.md)
