# AI Assist & Intelligence — features

**Product Map Item:** [`addon.aiAssist`](../../../../../product-map/addon/aiAssist.toon) · **Status:** ga · **Availability:** Billable add-on `INS-AO-AI-ASSIST`, offered from Standard (stages [agent](../../module/agent/index.md)); Advanced adds [intelligence](../../module/intelligence/index.md) (`offerings/standard.toon`, `offerings/advanced.toon`).

## What it is

AI Assist & Intelligence adds the Assistant agent on Standard and the embedded intelligence layer on Advanced. All assist is read-only or approval-gated.

## Who benefits

Analysts and authors who want guided help, under governance their security team can accept.
