# Anomaly Detection — features

**Product Map Item:** [`addon.anomalyDetection`](../../../../../product-map/addon/anomalyDetection.toon) · **Status:** ga · **Availability:** Billable add-on `INS-AO-ANOMALY-DETECTION`, offered from Standard and every higher Edition (`offerings/standard.toon`); it stages [anomaly](../../module/anomaly/index.md).

## What it is

Anomaly Detection scores records against learned models to flag unusual behaviour. Models are configured as `anomaly-model` and run as the `anomaly.score` job type.

## Who benefits

Fraud and assurance analysts looking for what fixed thresholds miss.
