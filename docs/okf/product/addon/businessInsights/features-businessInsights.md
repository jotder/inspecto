# Business Insights — features

**Product Map Item:** [`addon.businessInsights`](../../../../../product-map/addon/businessInsights.toon) · **Status:** Explain this change built (P2) · **Availability:** Billable add-on `INS-AO-BUSINESS-INSIGHTS`, offered from Standard and every higher Edition (`offerings/standard.toon`); it stages [insights](../../module/insights/index.md).

## What it is

On any Widget that charts a Measure over time, **Explain this change** tells the reader why the last period moved:
which values of which dimensions carry the change (the fewest that explain most of it), for a ratio how much came
from the mix shifting and how much from the rates themselves, and which other Measures moved together with it. It
reads only what the reader may already see, with the same masking as the Widget.

## Who benefits

Business owners and analysts who read Dashboards and need the reason behind a number before acting on it.

## Not yet

Pairs of dimensions (depth 2), Insight Profiles and the insight scan come with P3 of BI-INSIGHTS-1.
