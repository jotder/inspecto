# Multi-entity / Group — features

**Product Map Item:** [`addon.multiEntity`](../../../../../product-map/addon/multiEntity.toon) · **Status:** ga · **Availability:** Billable add-on `INS-AO-MULTI-ENTITY`, offered from Standard and every higher Edition (`offerings/standard.toon`); it stages [exchange](../../module/exchange/index.md).

## What it is

Multi-entity / Group lets several organisations or units exchange data through the exchange module. A background task keeps exchange snapshots fresh.

## Who benefits

Groups and regulators that combine assurance across several entities.
