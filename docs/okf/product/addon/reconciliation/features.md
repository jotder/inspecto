# Reconciliation — features

**Product Map Item:** [`addon.reconciliation`](../../../../../product-map/addon/reconciliation.toon) · **Status:** ga · **Availability:** Billable add-on `INS-AO-RECONCILIATION`, offered from Standard and every higher Edition (`offerings/standard.toon`); it stages [reconciliation](../../module/reconciliation/index.md).

## What it is

Reconciliation compares two sides of the same business — for example billed versus switched — and reports the gaps. Reconciliations are configured as a config kind and run as `recon.run`.

## Who benefits

Revenue assurance and finance teams.
