# Workflow & SLA — features

**Product Map Item:** [`addon.workflowSla`](../../../../../product-map/addon/workflowSla.toon) · **Status:** ga · **Availability:** Billable add-on `INS-AO-WORKFLOW-SLA`, offered from Standard and every higher Edition (`offerings/standard.toon`); it stages [ops](../../module/ops/index.md).

## What it is

Workflow & SLA adds lifecycles, SLA policies and escalation to operational objects. A background SLA sweep watches deadlines.

## Who benefits

Operations managers accountable for response times.

- `workflow`, `sla-policy` and `escalation-rule` config kinds plus the `sla-sweep` background task — [feature-db](feature-db.md)
