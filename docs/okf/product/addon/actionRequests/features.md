# Action Requests — features

**Product Map Item:** [`addon.actionRequests`](../../../../../product-map/addon/actionRequests.toon) · **Status:** ga · **Availability:** Billable add-on `INS-AO-ACTION-REQUESTS`, offered from Standard and every higher Edition (`offerings/standard.toon`); it stages [action-requests](../../module/action-requests/index.md).

## What it is

Action Requests let the platform ask an external system to do something — through the `invoke-api` consequence — and track the request. It turns a detection into a governed outbound action.

## Who benefits

Operations teams who want detections to trigger work in other systems with a trail.
