# Learning Path — Business user

You do not build Pipelines or Alert Rules: you read the Dashboards a builder made for you and work the Incidents and
Cases they raise. This is the short path — about two hours.

## Understand (about 45 minutes)

1. [How the pieces fit](user-guide-concept-overview.md)
2. [Dataset](user-guide-concept-dataset.md) · [Widget and Dashboard](user-guide-concept-widget-dashboard.md)
3. [Alert, Incident and Case](../../addon/incidents/user-guide-concept-alert-incident-case.md)
4. If your team investigates links between people, accounts or devices:
   [Link Analysis and Investigations](../../module/la-core/user-guide-concept-link-analysis.md) — the Link Analysis
   training videos go deeper.

## Read and respond (about 1 hour)

1. [Tutorial 4 — Present it: a Dashboard for the business user](user-guide-tutorial-dashboard.md) — read the
   Order-to-cash Dashboard as its business user does.
2. [Open and work a Case](../../module/case-management/user-guide-howto-open-and-work-a-case.md)
3. [Schedule a report](../../module/analytics/user-guide-howto-schedule-a-report.md) — when you want the numbers in
   your inbox.
4. Any screen you have not met yet: the [screen reference](../../../../USER_GUIDE.md).

## Self-check

1. The Dashboard shows a total unmatched value. Is it money owed to you, owed by you, or both?
2. What is the difference between an Incident and a Case?
3. A scheduled report arrives as a file. Is it a picture of the Dashboard?

## Self-check answers

1. Both: the unmatched value is the difference between what was ordered and what was paid, whichever way round — an
   unpaid order and a payment for no order both count.
2. An Incident is one thing that needs attention; a Case groups Incidents and evidence into one piece of work, with
   its own workflow.
3. No — it is an export of a Dataset, as CSV or Excel.
