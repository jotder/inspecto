# Feature modules — features

**Product Map Item:** [`layer.features`](../../../../../product-map/layer/features.toon) · **Status:** ga · **Availability:** Grouping layer; its modules are staged per Edition by `offerings/*.toon`.

## What it is

Feature modules are the optional business capabilities — scoring, screening, cases, backup and the like — that sit on the platform. Each one is installed by Edition or add-on, and absent modules leave nothing behind.

## What this enables

- Customers buy only the capabilities they need; how modules are classified and gated is in [architecture-module-taxonomy](../platform/architecture-module-taxonomy.md)
