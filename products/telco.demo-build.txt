INSPECTO DEMO BUILD (INSPECTO TELCO) - INTERNAL EVALUATION ONLY
===============================================================
This build signs people in as Demo Users with NO password and NO identity provider (DEMO-AUTH-1).
It is not secure and must never be given to a customer, exposed on a network, or used with real data.

UNZIP, RUN, SIGN IN. The Space telco is PRE-INSTALLED under .\spaces\telco: Revenue Assurance, Fraud Management and SIM-box
bypass over ONE usage feed (telecom_usage), with their synthetic July 2026 samples ingested and every App's Jobs run for each
day. Nothing needs a seed step; no node is required. What you will find:
  Revenue Assurance   the two Reconciliations (switch vs mediation vs rating; rated vs billed) and their breaks, the leakage and
                      data-quality findings (17 each) and their Alerts, the KPIs
  Fraud Management    the fourteen typologies (IRSF, Wangiri, SIM box, premium rate, roaming, SIM swap, ...), each with its
                      planted offenders and one Alert per typology; Incidents and Cases in Case Management
  SIM-box bypass      Risk Scores per SIM, and one suspected box: the cell C500, where FM's five premium-rate callers share a
                      cell (a finding no single App's data plants; kept on purpose)
  Link Analysis       explore any of those numbers as a graph

1. Run bin\serve-demo.bat (Windows) or bin/serve-demo.sh (Linux). Set PORT to change the port (default 8080).
2. Open http://127.0.0.1:8080 and pick a Demo User:
     admin        Demo Admin        Platform administrator: every area and setting
     ra.analyst   Demo RA Analyst   Revenue Assurance analyst: runs Jobs, works Incidents and Cases
     fm.analyst   Demo FM Analyst   Fraud analyst: runs Jobs, works Incidents and Cases
     fm.manager   Demo FM Manager   Fraud manager: also authors Alert Rules and Pipelines
     viewer       Demo Viewer       Non-privileged viewer: reads, changes nothing
3. Use the App switcher at the top of the rail to move between Revenue Assurance, Fraud Management, Case Management and
   Link Analysis. My work on the landing page lists the open Incidents and Cases.

The findings are dated July 2026, the corpus's month: filter by that period, not by "today".

RESET THE DEMO: stop the server, delete .\spaces\telco, and restore that folder from the zip (re-extract spaces\telco).
Anything done during a session (assignments, dispositions, new Rules) lives in that folder, so a reset discards it.

The server listens on 127.0.0.1 only and refuses to start the demo sign-in on any other address.
The bundled SBOM describes the Advanced module set: it lists inspecto-oidc (not shipped here) and
omits inspecto-demo-auth (shipped here).
